The 72-hour kit

Most help arrives within three days. Keep this ready in one bag by the door.

  • Water: 4 litres per person per day, for three days
  • Food that needs no cooking, and a manual can opener
  • Medicines for two weeks, copies of prescriptions, glasses
  • First-aid kit, and know how to stop bleeding
  • Torch, battery or wind-up radio, spare batteries, power bank
  • Cash in small notes: card machines fail when the grid does
  • Copies of passports and IDs: paper in a zip bag, and digital
  • Phone numbers written on paper, including one contact outside your region
  • Warm layer, rain cover, sturdy shoes, gloves
  • N95 or FFP2 masks, for smoke, ash and disease
  • Whistle, to signal if you are trapped
  • Sanitation: wet wipes, bin bags, water purification tablets
  • Spare keys for home and car; keep the car's tank at least half full
  • For children, older people and pets: their food, medicines and comfort items

The family plan

Ten minutes of talking now saves hours of panic later.

  • Agree two meeting points: one near home, one outside your neighbourhood
  • Pick one out-of-area contact everyone calls or texts to say they are safe
  • Know two ways out of your home and your street
  • Learn where to shut off gas, water and electricity
  • Find your nearest shelter, high ground and evacuation route
  • Sign up for your country's official alert app or SMS service
  • Text rather than call after a disaster: texts get through when networks jam
  • Foreign citizens: register with your embassy
