About CIV FM

We build instruments for the whole world.

CIV FM is a collective of builders, researchers and storytellers turning the planet's open knowledge into free tools anyone can hold: to see where they live, who governs them, what they are owed, and what is coming next.

One globe, every place on Earth

Mission

One living globe that shows any person, anywhere, the air they breathe, the rights their constitution promises, the people who hold power over them, and the century ahead of them. Every number sourced. Every tool free.

What we build

Instruments, not apps.

Each one turns a mountain of public data into something a person can read in a few seconds, then dig into for hours.
